About the Firm

A Legacy of Trusted Legal Advocacy

Founded and based in Bangalore, VRDI Law has grown into one of the city's respected boutique law firms. We combine deep Karnataka legal expertise with a client-first approach that puts your outcomes above all else.

Our advocates practise across the Karnataka High Court, City Civil Courts, and District Courts, with a network that extends to arbitration forums and regulatory bodies nationwide.
